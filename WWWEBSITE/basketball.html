Thangzor Basketball Ground

Samdrup Jongkhar, Deothang

The basketball ground at Jigme Namgyel Engineering College (JNEC) is a well-maintained space where students engage in regular sports activities, especially basketball. It serves as a key hub for promoting physical fitness, teamwork, and community spirit among the students.

The ground often hosts intra-college and inter-college tournaments, fostering healthy competition and offering students a break from their academic routine. During sports festivals or annual events, the field becomes a lively spot where crowds gather to cheer for their teams. It’s not only a place for competitive matches but also a venue for recreational play in the evenings or weekends, creating a balance between academic and extracurricular life.

Given its location in the serene environment of Dewathang, the field also benefits from the scenic backdrop of Bhutan's lush greenery and hills, making it a peaceful and refreshing spot for both players and spectators alike.
