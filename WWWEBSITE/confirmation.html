Thangzor Table Tennis Facilities

Samdrup Jongkhar, Deothang

Jigme Namgyel Engineering College (JNEC) also has facilities for table tennis. It is another popular indoor game among students and is often played in recreational halls or dedicated indoor spaces.

Table tennis provides a great way for students to unwind and sharpen their reflexes during breaks between classes or in the evenings. The college may organize friendly matches or small tournaments as part of its sports festivals or events. This sport, along with badminton, adds to the variety of indoor sports available on campus, giving students multiple options for physical activity and recreation.
